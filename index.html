About the Department of CSE (AIML)

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata, provides a state-of-the-art four-year B.Tech curriculum centered around deep learning, computational mathematics, cognitive systems, and autonomous robotics.

Our department is equipped with high-performance GPU computing clusters, dedicated AIML and IoT research laboratories, and maintains active collaborations with the IETE Student Forum, IEEE Computer Society, and the Institute Innovation Council (IIC).
